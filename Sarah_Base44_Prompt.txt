Build a mobile web app called Sarah's Fourteen.

Paste this whole prompt into Base44 and build it. Do not invent different meals, different cheat days, or a different shift calendar. The JSON at the bottom is the app. Use it exactly.

Who it is for
Sarah is a 40-year-old woman on a long shift rotation: 14 day shifts, then 14 days off, then 14 night shifts, then 14 days off, then it repeats. She wants simple food she can cook tired. High fibre on regular days. Food that gives her mood a floor: oats, beans, lentils, yogurt, fruit with the skin on, walnuts, chia, flax, and salmon a few times. No onions, no green onions, no onion powder. Nothing gross: no sardines, no anchovies, no liver, no jars of fermented cabbage. Store salsa and tomato soup only if the label has no onion.

What the app is
Sixty days. Three meals a day. One full phone screen per day. Big type she can read at the stove. A shopping list every 14 days, color coded by aisle, so she can walk the store without deciding.

The name on the screen is Sarah's Fourteen. The small line under it is Sixty days on the 14s.

Look
Background cream #F4EFE4. Text #241C14. Cards #FFFAF3.
Meal 1 gold #FFE08A. Meal 2 green #C7EFD4. Meal 3 blue #D5E3FF.
Tags: Brain #1D4E89, Calm #5C4D7A, Steady #9A5416, Gut #1B4332. White text on the tags.
Shift badges: day shift brown #9A5416, night shift blue #1D4E89, day off green #1B4332, cheat day pink #C73E54. White text.
Shop aisle colors, use these exact hex values from the data: Fruit #BC6C25, Produce #2D6A4F, Protein #1D4E89, Dairy #5C4D7A, Bread #9A5416, Cans #1B4332, Grains #8A6A2F, Frozen #3D5A80, Treats #C73E54.
Font like Lato, or a plain system font if that is easier. Meal names about 26px. Ingredients about 18px. One cooking line under each meal.
No stock photos. No illustrations of bowls. The color blocks are the graphic.
Phone width. One column. Large buttons a thumb can hit. Respect the bottom safe area so the nav is not under the home bar.
Do not use an em dash or an en dash in any interface copy.

Screens
Bottom nav with three buttons: Today, Days, Shop.

1. Today
Shows the current plan day. If she has not set a start, day 1.
Huge day number. Fibre total in a dark pill.
Shift badge and, when the day is a cheat day, a cheat badge.
A note, then the timing line from the data.
Three stacked cards, gold then green then blue.
Each card shows the meal role, when to eat it, the mood tag, the fibre grams, the recipe name, the ingredient lines, and the cook line.
Previous and Next under the cards. Previous is off on day 1. Next is off on day 60.
A number box and a button that says Make this today. If she types 12 and taps it, today becomes day 12, and tomorrow the app shows day 13 by itself. Store the start date on the phone.
Footer line: No onions. Food supports mood. It does not treat it.

2. Days
Title: Pick a day.
Filters: All, Day shifts, Nights, Off, Cheat.
Each row is one day. Show the day number, the fibre total, the timing line, and the three meal names on gold, green, and blue bars.
Tap a row and open that day on the cook screen.

3. Shop
Title: Shop without thinking.
Short line: One list per block. Color is the aisle. Check items off. The phone remembers.
Five trip cards. The left border color matches the shift: brown for day shifts, green for days off, blue for nights.
Each card shows the trip title, the blurb, and how many items are checked, like 4 of 22 checked.
Tap a trip to open it.

4. One shopping trip
A button back to all trips.
The trip title and blurb.
Aisles in this order: Fruit, Produce, Protein, Dairy, Bread, Cans, Grains, Frozen, Treats. Skip an aisle if that trip has nothing in it.
Each aisle name is colored with that aisle's hex.
Each item is a row with a checkbox, the food name in bold, and the amount. The left edge of the row uses the aisle color.
Checking an item crosses it out and stays checked if she closes the app.
If the trip has a still list, show a heading Still in the cupboard and the names. Under that: Do not buy these again unless the jar is empty.

Shift rules, already baked into the JSON
Do not recalculate the calendar. Read shift, blockDay, line, and the meal slot names from each day.
Day shift meals are Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner.
Night shift meals are Before shift, Mid shift, After shift. The first card is the big plate, eaten when she wakes, before work. The middle card is packed. The last card is small, then she sleeps.
Days off use Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, and the line says which day of the 14-day break it is.
Every day line includes this idea: stop caffeine about 6 hours before sleep. On nights that means before the daytime sleep.
The 60 days cover one full cycle of 56 days plus the first 4 day shifts of the next round.
Days 1 to 14 are day shifts. Days 15 to 28 are off. Days 29 to 42 are nights. Days 43 to 56 are off. Days 57 to 60 are day shifts again.

Cheat days, already marked in the JSON
Days 3, 13, 16, 27, 32, 39, 43, 54, and 59. One each week, not the same kind of day. Show the cheat badge. Do not add a lecture.

House rules to keep in the copy, not as a new essay screen
About 25 grams of fibre on a regular day. Cheat days are lower on purpose.
Fruit stays whole.
If beans are new to her, half portions the first week and water with the meal. Put that as one line on the shop screen, not on every day.
Food can give mood a floor. It does not treat anxiety or depression.

Build rules
Hardcode the JSON below. Do not call an API for the meals.
No accounts. No ads. No calorie target. No weight goal.
Save two things on the phone only: which day she set as today, and which shop boxes are checked.
Build the day screen first. It has to be readable while she cooks. Then the day list. Then the shop.
Sound like a person who cooks. Short sentences. No hype. No brochure language.
